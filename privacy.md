# Privacy Policy — HOTEL RP BOT

**Last updated:** July 30, 2026

## 1. What We Collect
The Bot collects and stores the following data:

- **Discord Server IDs** — to store server-specific settings and configurations
- **Discord User IDs** — for command cooldowns, user preferences, and moderation features
- **Channel IDs** — to know which channels to use for welcome/goodbye messages

## 2. What We Do NOT Collect
- Message content (beyond what is needed to process a command)
- Personal identifying information (names, emails, etc.)
- Voice data or images
- IP addresses or location data

## 3. How We Use Data
Data is used only to provide Bot functionality:
- Running commands and responding to interactions
- Maintaining server-specific settings
- Ensuring fair usage through cooldowns

## 4. Data Storage
Data is stored securely and is only accessible to the Bot operators. We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to third parties.

## 5. Data Retention
Data is retained as long as the Bot is in your server. If the Bot is removed from your server, associated data will be deleted within 30 days.

## 6. Your Rights
You may request deletion of your data by:
- Removing the Bot from your server (server-level data)
- Contacting us directly through GitHub issues

## 7. Third-Party Services
The Bot uses:
- Discord API (governed by Discord's Privacy Policy)
- Railway for hosting (governed by Railway's Privacy Policy)

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
